# What is Google Business Profile?

**Google Business Profile** (previously called **Google My Business**) is a free service by Google that allows businesses to create an online profile. It helps businesses appear on **Google Search** and **Google Maps**, making it easier for customers to find contact details, location, business hours, and reviews.

## Advantages of Google Business Profile

1. Increases online visibility.
2. Appears on Google Search and Google Maps.
3. Helps customers find your business easily.
4. Displays business hours, phone number, and address.
5. Customers can leave ratings and reviews.
6. Improves trust and credibility.
7. Supports photos, products, and services.
8. Free to use.
