# What is a Website?

A **website** is a collection of related web pages that are stored on a web server and accessed through a web browser using a domain name or URL.

**Example:** `https://www.google.com`, `https://www.wikipedia.org`

---

# Types of Websites

## 1. Static Website

* Displays fixed content.
* Content changes only when edited by the developer.
* **Example:** Portfolio website, company profile website.

## 2. Dynamic Website

* Content changes automatically based on user interaction or database data.
* **Example:** Facebook, Amazon, YouTube.

## 3. E-commerce Website

* Used to buy and sell products online.
* **Example:** Amazon, Flipkart.

## 4. Business Website

* Provides information about a company, its products, and services.
* **Example:** Company websites.

## 5. Educational Website

* Provides learning materials, online courses, and study resources.
* **Example:** School, college, or university websites.

## 6. Blog Website

* Used to publish articles, news, opinions, and personal experiences.
* **Example:** Personal blogs and technology blogs.

## 7. News Website

* Shares the latest news and current events.
* **Example:** News portals.

## 8. Social Networking Website

* Allows users to connect, chat, and share photos, videos, and posts.
* **Example:** Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn.

## 9. Portfolio Website

* Showcases a person's work, skills, and achievements.
* **Example:** Designer or photographer portfolio.

## 10. Government Website

* Provides government information and online public services.
* **Example:** Government department websites.

## 11. Entertainment Website

* Offers movies, music, games, and videos.
* **Example:** YouTube, Netflix.

## 12. Personal Website

* Created by an individual to share personal information, hobbies, or achievements.
* **Example:** Personal profile website.

---

# Advantages of a Website

1. Available 24×7.
2. Helps share information worldwide.
3. Promotes businesses and services.
4. Supports online shopping and payments.
5. Improves communication with customers.
6. Easy to update and maintain.
7. Builds trust and brand awareness.
