# What is WWW?

**WWW (World Wide Web)** is a collection of web pages and websites that are connected through the Internet. It allows users to access information using a web browser.

**Example:** When you open a website like `https://www.google.com`, you are using the **World Wide Web (WWW)**.

> **Note:** The **Internet** is the network that connects computers worldwide, while the **WWW** is the collection of websites and web pages that run on the Internet.

## Why is WWW Used in the Web?

WWW is used to make information available and accessible over the Internet. It helps users easily browse websites and access online services.

### Uses of WWW

1. **To access websites** – Open and view web pages through a browser.
2. **To search information** – Find educational, business, and general information.
3. **Communication** – Use email, social media, and online messaging.
4. **Online learning** – Access e-learning platforms, tutorials, and study materials.
5. **E-commerce** – Shop online and make digital payments.
6. **Entertainment** – Watch videos, listen to music, and play online games.
7. **Online banking** – Check account details and transfer money securely.
8. **Business and Marketing** – Promote products and services online.

## Features of WWW

* Easy to access through a web browser.
* Uses **HTTP** or **HTTPS** protocols.
* Supports text, images, audio, video, and animations.
* Connects web pages using hyperlinks.
* Available 24×7 from anywhere with an Internet connection.

## Advantages of WWW

* Quick access to information.
* Easy communication worldwide.
* Supports online education.
* Enables online shopping and banking.
* Helps businesses reach customers globally.
* Saves time and effort.
