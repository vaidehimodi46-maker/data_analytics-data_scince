# SEO (SEARCH ENGINE OPTIMIZATION)
## DEFINITION
    SEO stands for Search Engine Optimization.

    It is the process of improving a website to increase its ranking and visibility in search engine results such as Google.

    The main goal of SEO is to attract more organic (unpaid) traffic.

# TYPES OF SEO
1.ON-PAGE SEO
    Improving content and elements inside a website.

    Examples:

    Keywords
    Page titles
    Headings
    Meta descriptions
    Quality content
2.OFF-PAGE SEO
    Activities performed outside the website to improve ranking.

    Examples:

    Backlinks
    Social media promotion
    Guest blogging
3.TECHNICAL SEO
    Improving the technical performance of a website.

    Examples:

    Website speed
    Mobile-friendly design
    HTTPS
    XML sitemap
# ADVANTAGES OF SEO
    Increases website visibility.
    Brings organic traffic.
    Builds brand awareness.
    Improves trust and credibility.
    Improves user experience.
    Increases leads and sales.
    Provides long-term benefits.
    Helps compete with other websites.
# SIMPLE DEFINITION
    SEO is the process of optimizing a website to improve its search engine ranking and attract more organic traffic.
