# What is SEO?

**SEO (Search Engine Optimization)** is the process of improving a website so that it ranks higher in search engine results, such as Google. The main goal of SEO is to increase the number of visitors to a website through **organic (free)** search results.

**Example:** If a website is optimized for the keyword **"best mobile phones"**, it has a better chance of appearing on the first page of Google search results.

## Types of SEO

### 1. On-Page SEO

* Optimizing the content and structure of web pages.
* Includes keywords, headings, images, meta tags, and internal links.

### 2. Off-Page SEO

* Activities performed outside the website to improve its ranking.
* Includes backlinks, social media promotion, and guest posting.

### 3. Technical SEO

* Improves the technical performance of a website.
* Includes website speed, mobile-friendliness, HTTPS security, XML sitemap, and fixing crawl errors.

## Advantages of SEO

1. Increases website traffic.
2. Improves search engine ranking.
3. Builds trust and credibility.
4. Attracts the right audience.
5. Provides long-term results.
6. Increases brand awareness.
7. Improves user experience.
8. Cost-effective compared to paid advertising.
9. Increases leads and sales.
10. Gives a competitive advantage.

## Example of SEO

Suppose you own an online cosmetic store.

* **Without SEO:** Your website may appear on page 5 of Google, so very few people visit it.
* **With SEO:** Your website can appear on page 1 for keywords like **"best lipstick"** or **"face cream"**, bringing more visitors and potential customers.
