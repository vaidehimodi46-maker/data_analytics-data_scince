# What are LAN, MAN, and WAN?

**LAN, MAN, and WAN** are different types of computer networks. They are classified based on the geographical area they cover.

---

# 1. LAN (Local Area Network)

A **LAN (Local Area Network)** is a network that connects computers and devices within a **small area**, such as a home, school, office, or laboratory.

### Features

* Covers a small area.
* High data transfer speed.
* Easy to install and maintain.
* Usually owned by one organization.

**Example:** Computers connected in a school computer lab or office.

---

# 2. MAN (Metropolitan Area Network)

A **MAN (Metropolitan Area Network)** is a network that connects multiple LANs within a **city or metropolitan area**.

### Features

* Covers a city or large town.
* Faster than WAN but slower than LAN.
* Connects different offices or campuses in the same city.

**Example:** A university connecting all its campuses within a city.

---

# 3. WAN (Wide Area Network)

A **WAN (Wide Area Network)** is a network that connects computers over a **large geographical area**, such as different cities, countries, or continents.

### Features

* Covers a very large area.
* Connects multiple LANs and MANs.
* Used for long-distance communication.
* The Internet is the largest WAN.

**Example:** Bank branches connected across India.

---

# Difference Between LAN, MAN, and WAN

| Feature       | LAN                          | MAN                           | WAN                    |
| ------------- | ---------------------------- | ----------------------------- | ---------------------- |
| Full Form     | Local Area Network           | Metropolitan Area Network     | Wide Area Network      |
| Coverage Area | Small (Room, Office, School) | City or Metropolitan Area     | Country or Worldwide   |
| Speed         | High                         | Medium                        | Lower than LAN and MAN |
| Cost          | Low                          | Medium                        | High                   |
| Example       | School Lab                   | University Campuses in a City | Internet, Bank Network |

---

# Advantages

### LAN

* High speed.
* Low cost.
* Easy file and printer sharing.
* Secure network.

### MAN

* Connects multiple LANs.
* Covers an entire city.
* Suitable for universities and organizations.

### WAN

* Connects computers worldwide.
* Supports long-distance communication.
* Enables global data sharing.

---

# Easy Definition (Exam Answer)

### LAN (Local Area Network)

A LAN is a network that connects computers within a small area such as a home, school, or office.

### MAN (Metropolitan Area Network)

A MAN is a network that connects multiple LANs within a city or metropolitan area.

### WAN (Wide Area Network)

A WAN is a network that connects computers over a large geographical area, such as different cities, countries, or the entire world.

**Examples:**

* **LAN:** School computer lab
* **MAN:** University campuses in one city
* **WAN:** Internet, bank branch network
